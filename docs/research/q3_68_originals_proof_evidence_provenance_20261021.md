### Reconciliation and specific scientific FAIL boundaries

- **Proven bounded mechanism:** source-conditioned known algebra, program invariants and exact candidate checking can sometimes convert generic SMT solver UNKNOWN within an arbitrary fixed wall budget into a certified actual original-task SAT/UNSAT/SAFE/UNSAFE answer. That is genuine and useful bounded evidence for classical hybrid capability. No trick of weakening the original target is allowed.
- **NOT proven:** a *trained neural model* discovers a new useful invariant that equally tool-equipped strongest classical expert cannot discover; complete iso-capability Q4/Q5 cost advantage (especially including training, retries, cold-start and theorem/source retrieval); Q6 generalization to genuinely new authors/families *for a fixed trained system*; Q7 frontier-level gap. Prior v102 learned-support is separate historical bounded evidence; do not overwrite it.
- **Against best Direct:** direct specialists are permitted the exact same symbolic/rank/parity/arithmetic/theorem library. When so equipped, none of these 68 source results establishes a **unique NEUMANN** model capability or 10x full-system efficiency. Raw generic Z3+cvc5 alone is a weaker comparator: its deficits are not sufficient to close Q3–Q7.
- **Stop task saturation as fake proof mining:** both the new Uncu 24/24 generic-solver-saturated family and finally adjudicated Code2Inv24 (classical 24/24) have no authentic unsolved truth left. Replaying more similar cases to grow raw proof-count is NOT the remaining bottleneck.
- **SC03:** deep open 3x3 semi-magic cubes mathematical problem family. **SQ03:** retained UNKNOWN, not incorrectly called SAT/UNSAT. Do not pressure a small 4B/CPU model to magically prove current open number theory or misattribute known theorems to training.
- **Decision change:** the next model experiment must be preregistered *before* training and heldout original content. Select structurally new programs with non-vacuous true SAFE or constructive SAT gold-free verifier where strongest expert portfolio (including BMC, SyGuS, Spacer, known algebra/theorem retrieval, same source size/time/authority) actually fails, then train a CPU-eligible, typed structure-proposing model and compare with same-model direct executable, no-learn, and no-compression ablations. Output only exact independently original-verified extra solved cases, full wall/compute/inference/check/training cost and holdout family effects. If no genuine residual, do not train or mark Q3 passed.
- **Historical frozen failure preservation:** original PR371 18/24 SAFE and remaining unknowns remains first evidence; late 19/5 is POSTHOC integrated proof, not a retuned first-blind run. The original 18 benchmark family 13/18 raw Direct remains first evidence though all 18 were later classified with mathematical prior-art.

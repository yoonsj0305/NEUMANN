## Decision
A new typed interpreter can solve some old source-consensus impossibility cases, but this does not make an intelligent model grow through verified experience. **The missing mechanism is learned generalization over HOW to create an executable semantic operator from failures**, not further manually appended date/decimal primitives or the ability to choose from an existing DSL. Training a fixed-action neural ranker on 3 independently verified repair episodes is scientifically underdetermined; do not spend until genuinely independent repair trajectories, same-rights strong classical comparison, A/sham ablation, and full costs are available. Keep classical-first and fail-closed. Global Q1–Q7 OPEN.

Recent relevant research context: POPL 2026 `Inductive Program Synthesis by Meta-Analysis-Guided Hole Filling` (DOI 10.1145/3776694) shows semantic pruning/hole selection improvements can often remain classical; 2026 `Property-Guided LLM Program Synthesis for Planning` (arXiv:2605.16142) highlights verifiable properties for targeted feedback rather than blindly adding generation attempts. We take narrow principles, not unsupported performance equivalence.

## Receipt interface

`neumann1.frontier_gap_contract.audit` accepts:

- A prepublished manifest fixing task original hashes/families, three system
  identities, repetitions, verifier identity, shared tool authority, capability
  thresholds and resource-ratio thresholds, source/license declarations, call/cost/
  deadline caps and cold/investment accounting. No defaults decide scientific success.
- Complete receipt coverage for small, frontier and NEUMANN on every registered
  task/repeat, including failed answers. Each receipt binds original problem,
  final answer and raw execution trace to content hashes and the manifest.
- Raw retained original tasks, answers and traces keyed by SHA256.
- Trusted independent original-task verification and execution-trace validation
  callbacks. They return actual Boolean outcomes; the audit does not trust a
  receipt's `accepted` flag or execute a model.

Every execution trace must retain all stages, attempts, routing, verification,
retry/fallback and complete costs. The callback is responsible for validating
provider/process receipts, deadline behavior and all attempted work. Hashes prove
content identity, NOT that a provider executed a model, independent verification,
prepublication chronology or absence of contamination. Those require external
provenance/audit. Synthetic unit fixtures are never actual Frontier Gap evidence.

## Resource surface and conservative decisions

Six explicit axes: compute, peak RAM, peak VRAM, complete latency, energy and
monetary cost. Compute has one frozen metric/unit; heterogeneous CPU/GPU/token
quantities cannot be added without a defensible model. RAM/VRAM use bytes,
latency milliseconds, energy joules and cost the frozen currency.

Each measurement carries `measured`, `estimated` or `unavailable`, an exact unit,
and a raw trace hash. Failed routes retain all available cost measurements.
Actual inference cost must include local AND remote work; cold/investment
amortization and deployment traffic conventions belong in the manifest.

Only wholly measured, compatible per-role complete totals can produce a measured
resource ratio. Estimated data is visible but does not become a measured claim;
unavailable data never becomes zero. A hosted API's closed FLOPs/VRAM/energy may
remain unavailable even if token usage, wall latency and invoice cost are known.
Such an experiment can establish a bounded capability/cost result but cannot
establish all North Star resource claims.

Gap capability and resource thresholds are checked overall AND per represented
family; a favorable family cannot conceal another family's failure. RAM/VRAM
aggregate as peaks, other axes as complete per-query means. All frozen
repeat failures count. Empty/undersized gap families do not prove Q6. Even a
fully favorable receipt contract reports globalQ1–Q7 OPEN: external provenance,
statistics, unseen conditions, open-set and multiple independent domains require
separate evidence. The audit result is a CASE-SET diagnostic, not global closure.

This three-role interface addresses a frontier comparator, not every possible
strong Direct strategy. Q5 also requires the best eligible Direct baselines and
component ablations at matched capability; a frontier-relative saving alone does
not close it. Offline receipt replay is not execution: runtime verification costs
must already be charged in each independently validated execution trace.

# NEUMANN 1 | G1/Q3 gate admission repaired, 2026-10-23

**Decision:** Existing bounded learning experiments have created and verified novel *derived* symbolic/recursive programs on public authored sources, but NO global neural-exclusive structural intelligence over strong classical and NO frontier 10× cost dominance. Q3 global **OPEN**, G1 **HOLD**.

## The concrete logical bottleneck

If a learner emits a program in a declared finite AST language `L`, and the classical arm actually enumerates every member of `L` with the identical native whole-function grader, the learned correct set must be a **subset** of the classical correct set. Repeating, increasing the model, or creating new corpus shards cannot result in any learner-exclusive program in that setup. This is a no-go for a **bad G1 test design**, NOT a proof of general AI inability.

**Scope warning:** a classical full-*action* sweep may be a superset when every action is independently evaluated, but a greedy top-360 pair fitter over 264 primitives is NOT full enumeration of all allowed programs. Explicitly label nonexhaustive baselines. Do not claim an unattainable neural win over complete identical-language solver.

**2026-10-20 KST | Truthful actual finding: `Q3_NO_FULL_GRAMMAR_ONLINE_POLICY=FAIL_NO_SPARSE_POLICY_ADVANTAGE`; GLOBAL Q3 OPEN.** This is the **one decisive** post-PR #300 trial; stop using this one source family to tune policy hyperparameters.

## Why this matters

In PR #300 the trainable 125-logit shape-conditional AST decoder emitted a correct compound modulo formula without receiving correct AST token labels; however its behavioral reward posterior required exhaustive scoring of **all 25,920 DSL programs**, so ordinary classical argmin had already solved the task and was cheaper. This trial completely removes the exhaustive precomputed reward distribution. The stochastic 125-parameter conditional grammar policy proposes one candidate at a time and updates weights using **only that candidate's actual source-reconstruction loss**, with 32 seeded repetitions under a strict compute/proposal cap.

## Fixed experimental semantics and no benchmark misrepresentation

- Same **already-known author's synthetic counter recurrence**, k=2..6 for 48 states per k, 240 source-trace observations, no external author-family-blind G1 source. Program's gold modulo formula and correct AST are never supplied as a label.
- AST DSL: 5 binary tree shapes×4³ typed arithmetic operators from {+,-,*, integer div}×3⁴ leaves from {a,b,1}, total **25,920**. `mod` NOT a direct grammar primitive. All successful modulo source ASTs are nevertheless **inside** the enumerated finite grammar (2 solutions among full 25,920); not evidence of invention outside supplied grammar.
- **No full-grammar pre-evaluation in online proposal policy.** Exactly **2,500** candidate source-feedback evaluations before freezing the 125-parameter categorical stochastic policy, then **500** further sampled proposals with frozen weights; all rewards from the authentic source traces. After each 125-candidate feedback batch, 7 actual NumPy analytic Adam gradient updates on behavioral soft-elite categorical log-likelihood (140 updates total). Source behavior loss `log1p(mean absolute error)+0.1×(1−exact-state-fraction)`, reward temperature 0.3, lr 0.22, uniform 30% exploration mix. These hyperparameters had been evaluated in **local development** before CI and are NOT preregistered blind.
- Fixed paired seeds `1,2,...,32`. Same budget **3,000 evaluated AST candidates per seed** for random-uniform full DSL comparator, independent RNG stream seed + 100000. Complete **32/32 rows** retained, even the failures. Each arm executed **96,000 AST behavior evaluations** across 32 seeds. The learned arm evaluated 69k–80k **distinct** AST candidates (2167–2503 per seed) because it repeatedly sampled them; the random baseline is just 96k independent draws from the complete grammar, not fixed no-replacement exhaustive enumeration.
- Estimated theoretical probability random-with-replacement finds at least one of 2 correct ASTs within 3000 tries is `1−(1−2/25920)^3000≈20.665%`. Actual observed random hit rate 4/32=12.5% is plausible with sampling variance. Observed relative 1/32 vs 4/32 is not proof that random is generally superior; the sample size is 32 and the 95% Wilson intervals overlap.

## Decision and architectural diagnosis

At a matched 3,000-proposal budget, this particular 125-parameter sparse-policy update failed to obtain any first frozen-weight AST discovery on seeds that had not already found a correct expression before freezing, and did worse than a uniform random benchmark in observed successes (1 vs 4).

**Do NOT iterate more hyperparameters on this same synthetic counter family**. Severe discrete reward sparsity and shape/operand credit assignment remain; frequency-gradient over partial source feedback over-rewards cheap near misses and prematurely contracts diverse program distributions. This is a plausible diagnosis, not a formally proven unique cause; a different policy, multi-task training corpus or typed partial-semantic credit assignment may behave differently.

**Next scientifically worthwhile gate:** Train a conditional reusable operator/AST prior across several **source-family-disjoint training programs** (not repeated mod counter k values); freeze model weights and generation contract before selecting untouched external test sources; require true original independent Z3/cvc5 three VCs on at least 2 independent author-origin families, full same-information exact classical CEGIS/enumerative/specialist comparisons, model ablation, no hidden target AST labels, and all-in training + proposal + verifier cost. If no learned advantage, retain classical composition as NEUMANN 1 mechanism and mark learned-exclusive novelty FAIL for that bounded family. No more synthetically manufactured proof as global Q3.

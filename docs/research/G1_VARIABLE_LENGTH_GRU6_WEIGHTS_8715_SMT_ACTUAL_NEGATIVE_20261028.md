## Why this is a useful **negative architecture result**

A variable-length compiler output head is **necessary but not sufficient** for OOD compositionality. No training sample in d1..3 offers a positive NEXT at the position after exactly three multiplication operators; teacher forcing either has EOS there or masks later positions entirely. The decoder cannot be assumed to learn `repeat operator until source pattern changes` from stop-token supervision alone. Actual unseen d4 gave only 2,3 or overflow7, never degree4. Deep AST RNN also had poor in-family coefficient and degree joint accuracy (~5–7% vs previous 80% fixed output heads) and training cost 176.88 CPU.

**Next G1 condition:** train only when source problems genuinely require new compositional structures and robust causally learned discovery policy. Hard-code grammar/semantic validity and allow *goal-conditioned incremental search* that changes policy through verified counterexamples, compare to strong same-right classical (CVC5/SMT/PDR/specialist) on independent-author unopened problems and fully charge training/verifier/failed attempts. Our toy C has full exact source task coverage and minimal computation; **STOP investing in recurrent AST length generator on this toy without new problem-domain evidence**.

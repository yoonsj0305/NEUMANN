## 0. Mission in one sentence

Build a HYBRID STRUCTURAL INTELLIGENCE ENGINE capable of recovering genuinely frontier-level problem-solving ability with the MINIMUM NECESSARY COMPLETE COMPUTATION, using classical deterministic algorithms for derivable work and a small learned component only where it discovers useful, previously unavailable goal-sufficient structure that earns its full cost.

NOT the objective: the smallest possible LM, another LLM router, parser optimization, generic tool orchestration, an LP-specific trick, artificial 10x on a toy metric, many successful CI tests, or having a pretty user interface.

10x or greater iso-capability economic advantage is the ambitious research TARGET, not an already demonstrated result or an automatic certification threshold. Source-group independence, external verification and complete resource denominators are more important than headline ratios.

## 1. Final architecture decision: HYBRID STRUCTURAL INTELLIGENCE

    ORIGINAL TASK + INTENDED GOAL + TRUSTED CONSTRAINTS
                    |
           source-bound semantic interpretation
                    |
           typed, goal-bound representation / IR
                    |
        cheap tests + strongest applicable Native first
                    |
         [only if proven opportunity exists]
       small LEARNED hypothesis about useful latent
       structure / sufficient coordinates / rewrites
                    |
      source-bound equivalence/sufficiency obligations
                    |
         independent verifier of ORIGINAL problem
                    |
      cheapest sufficient specialist executor chosen
                    |
       independently verify ORIGINAL final answer
                    |
         verified answer | fallback | abstain
                    |
       complete end-to-end receipts + safe reuse

The classical solver/SMT/CAS/DP/LP/SyGuS engine is a PRIMARY component, not an enemy. The learned component never issues a correctness certificate or obtains execute permission by writing "accepted=true".

PERCEIVE: relate goal/variables/constraints; REFRAME: propose a goal-sufficient representation, possibly a certified rewrite sequence; DISCRIMINATE: reject bad propositions cheaply; INTERNALIZE: retain proof-scoped structures and failures for matching sources. These are working research hypotheses, NOT proven general AI properties.

A cheap native solution should be executed directly. A structural proposer is admitted only where its CONDITIONAL marginal gain in capability or all-in economics is greater than the strongest already-eligible classical hybrid. Fallback and abstention are valid behavior but not free.

Edge and Cloud must share semantics/proof contracts; only available compute, budget, process residency, latency, thermal/energy and escalation rules vary. Classical CPU wheel is the base profile. Archived frozen neural checkpoints remain optional source-research mode until rights/packaging and real costs are solved. No implicit remote model call.

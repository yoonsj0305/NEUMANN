# G0 first — strong baselines remove the apparent primitive headroom

2026-10-06. **COMPLETE / G0_NO_G1_TRAINING_ADMISSION** for the registered
true-twin graph and low-rank SPD matrix candidates. No learned model, GPU,
training, frontier call or sealed payload. North Star/Q1–Q7 and prior verdicts
unchanged. This result does not veto other representations or all LPS research.

## Registration and first evidence

`docs/experiments/g0_headroom.preregister.json` was frozen before full-grid
generation/timing, SHA256
`1fb9fe822569cd96a7f05e2d3055c6a5ec4f199b2606f88f37a91db579265399`.
Source and dependency identities plus both D0 manifests are pinned. Tiny
correctness/negative-control tests were allowed development before registration;
they supplied no measured full-grid headroom.

24 constructed opened-development cases, 12 per domain; four routes per case,
one retained warmup and three measured repetitions: **384 observations, all 384
accepted**. Whole run 113.659 seconds, including worker launches, preparation,
reference work and storage. This is experiment wall time, not the sum of query
latencies or an energy/FLOPs measure.

Graph: latent cores 12/16, positive integer vertex weights, true-twin multiplicity
4/8/16, relabeled full graphs of 48–256 vertices. Routes: NetworkX exact weighted
branch-and-bound; original-graph HiGHS MILP; discovered true-twin quotient; free
correct twin classes. Positive weights permit merging true twins by summing
weights. Original clique feasibility and optimum are checked, not just the
reduced instance's answer.

Matrix: SPD A=I+UU', dimensions 512/1024/2048, ranks 4/16, two seeds. Routes:
SciPy positive-definite solve; CG; LAPACK pivoted Cholesky discovery plus
Woodbury solve; free correct U plus Woodbury. Only A/b enters non-oracle methods.
Residual <=1e-7*max(1,||b||) checks the original task. The registered family's
lambda_min(A)>=1 also bounds absolute solution error by the residual.

Primary ratio is the fastest **correct declared comparator median** divided by
the free-structure median. Gate: every case evaluable, >=10 geometric-mean ratio
per domain and >=3/4 of each domain's largest cases at >=10. This threshold is
now frozen for THIS contract; it does not modify historical or global criteria.

| Domain | Fastest comparator | Free-structure geometric mean advantage | Largest cases >=10x | Decision |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Graph | Known true-twin quotient, 12/12 cases | 1.0772x | 0/4 | No training investment for this candidate |
| Matrix | Existing CG, 12/12 cases | 1.1287x | 0/4 | No training investment for this candidate |

The free graph structure beats NetworkX without quotient reduction by 47.57x
geometric mean. That comparison alone would exaggerate NEUMANN's remaining
opportunity: the already available structural comparator reduces the ratio to
1.08x. The analogous Cholesky-only matrix ratio is 2.15x. Those weaker comparison
ratios are diagnostics, not an admission criterion.

## Verification, complete-cost boundary and sensitivity

`Continuation/G0_FIRST`: unchanged first inputs (NPZ public+Oracle fields),
registration, all witnesses/stage costs, warmups, events and file hashes.
`Continuation/G0_PREPARATION/first-result-receipt.json` independently pins first
manifest/report/replay. Existing model-free replay rechecks all 384 witnesses,
public hashes and original frozen verdict without new optimization/model calls.

`Continuation/G0_ANALYSIS`: independently rederives all **12 original graph
optima**. It groups original closed adjacency rows, checks all original internal
and cross-class blocks, and exhaustively enumerates every class subset (up to
65,536). It uses no construction labels. Integer optima agree with the original
MILP references. This is additional verification work, not replacement results.

Primary query latency charges public input scan/hash, transform/discovery,
execution/reconstruction and original-task verification. Failed attempts would
be retained; none occurred. A first-query cache is empty for every arm; the warm
exact-repeat cache floor is separately retained with its actual earlier discovery
route/time. No repeat-cache advantage is attributed to learned intelligence.

Imports, worker launch, generation/storage, independent offline references and
correct Oracle preparation are retained as experiment investment, excluded from
the deliberately optimistic primary query ratio. Free discovery is NOT deployment
cost. Upstream algorithm investment, energy, FLOPs and money are UNKNOWN. No full
resource Pareto or North-Star claim follows from this screen.

Removing fingerprint scan for a **post-run diagnostic only** gives geometric
means 1.12x (graph) and 3.57x (matrix); none of the 24 cases reaches 10x even under
that diagnostic. It neither changes the original gate nor proves unavoidable
scan costs for every future architecture.

These graphs have small latent cores and the matrices are constructed low-rank
instances. Neither is a frontier capability evaluation. Twelve cases/three
timing repeats support bounded investment screening, not universal statistics.
Other transformations/distributions may have headroom; no globally cheapest
representation was proved.

## Decision and next architecture compression

Do not train a neural true-twin selector or low-rank selector on this evidence.
Keep their cheap deterministic implementations as building blocks and strong
comparators. No new Gemma/MiniLM/model-size swap is justified by this outcome.

One prospective core remains: **cost-aware synthesis of composed representation
programs with explicit semantic preservation**, rather than selecting a single
known reduction. It must learn/generate bindings, compositions and reusable
abstraction definitions, reject false analogies, and beat symbolic search and a
fixed representation selector under matching permissions and investment budgets.
It is a hypothesis, NOT implemented learned LPS and NOT admitted to training.

The existing e-graph engine is reused for the symbolic comparator instead of
rewritten: `neumann1/perspective_symbolic_baseline.py`, egglog 14.0.0 MIT,
Windows wheel/native/library hashes and license in
`Continuation/EGRAPH_REUSE_PREPARATION`. It already accepts typed exact-integer
ASTs, generates/factors equivalent compositions, and checks equivalence under
declared ring axioms. Three correctness/negative-control tests pass; this is
baseline infrastructure only. Extracted tree size is NOT execution FLOPs or
proof of speedup. Floating-point ring rewrites are explicitly rejected.

[egglog](https://github.com/egraphs-good/egglog-python) supplies equality-saturation
infrastructure. [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) and
[Stitch](https://github.com/mlb2251/stitch) already address learned abstractions
and program libraries; composition/library generation alone is not a novelty
claim. DreamCoder/Stitch were researched here, not installed or experimentally
outperformed. Future novelty and speed must be measured against relevant strong
implementations and the same original tasks.

Next mainline work: a new BEFORE-RUN contract for genuinely expensive composition
discovery, with original-goal checks, false-analogy controls and strong symbolic
optimization. No broad new dataset or GPU training until headroom is demonstrated.
G1/G2 remain unexecuted; old Decision3 remains sealed.

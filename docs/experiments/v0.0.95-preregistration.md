# v0.0.95 — frozen graph incremental-value diagnostic

## Question

v0.0.94 showed that the frozen CG5 point selector and the compact graph route
start from the same retained 2m column set. The graph therefore cannot justify
itself by shortlist coverage alone. Its only admissible incremental value on
this fixed target is to reorder that same set into a materially better exact-m
basis proposal before restricted/native execution.

This version asks one narrow question before any new fit, holdout, or timing:
**does the already-frozen compact graph add enough basis information over the
same point-only selector to justify another paid graph experiment?**

## Frozen evidence and authority

Use only the retained v0.0.94 first probe archive and its exact v0.0.88 parent
training sources. No model inference, optimizer, solver call, timing rerun,
new inputs, checkpoint selection, or parameter sweep is permitted. The label
basis is used only offline for paired information diagnostics. Original-LP
certificate acceptance remains the primary authority because multiple optimal
bases may exist.

For each seed 87001 and 87002 and all 48 opened training inputs:

- point-only and compact must retain exactly the same 2m column set,
- compare original-certificate acceptance of their exact-m basis proposals,
- compare hidden-label basis recall `|proposal ∩ label| / m`,
- count paired cases where compact gains, ties, or loses at least one labelled
  basis column.

The contaminated v0.0.94 timing archive is not used for any speed claim.

## Frozen admission gate

A seed passes only if all of the following hold:

1. compact exact-m original-certificate count exceeds point-only by at least
   **4 of 48** cases;
2. mean paired hidden-label basis-recall gain is at least **+0.05** absolute;
3. compact improves labelled-basis hits on at least **12 of 48** cases;
4. compact loses labelled-basis hits on at most **6 of 48** cases;
5. the retained 2m sets are identical on **48 of 48** cases.

Both seeds must pass. Passing admits only a separately preregistered clean
complete-cost graph-refinement experiment. It does not admit new training,
fresh holdout use, Q3 closure, or Q4 closure.

Failure decision:
`STOP_GRAPH_REFINEMENT_INFORMATION_CANDIDATE`.

Success decision:
`ADMIT_CLEAN_GRAPH_REFINEMENT_COST_PROTOCOL_NOT_Q3_Q4`.

Native remains the default and global Q3/Q4 remain OPEN regardless of this
diagnostic. The thresholds are frozen before the first v0.0.95 replay.

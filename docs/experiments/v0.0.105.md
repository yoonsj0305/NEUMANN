## 3. Pure receipt contract

`neumann1/frontier_gap_contract.py` imports standard-library modules only and
does not call providers, solvers or neural models. A manifest explicitly fixes
three distinct system identities/revisions, equal eligible tools, original-task
hashes/families/source/license, repetitions, capability/resource thresholds,
call/cost/deadline caps and cold/investment accounting.

Complete task×role×repeat receipts include failures. Trusted original-task
checker and complete execution-provenance adapters have no defaults. The gap
comes ONLY from independently checked small/frontier outcomes; NEUMANN cannot
select membership. Verifier crashes are not proof of baseline failure.

Capabilities and resource thresholds are checked overall AND per family. Six
axes distinguish measured/estimated/unavailable data, preserve complete failed
attempt costs and forbid unknown=zero or tokens=FLOPs claims. Peak RAM/VRAM is
not summed across sequential queries. No ratio is emitted without iso-capability
and wholly measured compatible quantities; zero denominators remain unknown.

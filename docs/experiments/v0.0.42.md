# v0.0.42 — Nonunit visibility and conflict-aware residual peeling

Status: pre-registered before implementation and before final data evaluation.
No learned block model is trained.

## Question

v0.0.41 found two distinct failures on generated exact linear systems:

- generic nonunit block targets had 0% visibility to the unit-only parser;
- in the overlap arm, declared targets were 100% visible but D1/D2 selected
  incompatible candidate sets and fell back to full solving on 64/64 cases.

Can a simple deterministic parser and a residual-incidence selector close
those gaps while retaining original-problem verification? This is an explicit
test of an engineered motif; it is not a general structure-discovery test.

## Frozen methods

Keep v0.0.41 D0-D4 unchanged as baselines. Both new methods use the existing
frozen incidence-one local router, exact 2x2 derivation and algebra checker,
joint materializer, and original-system verifier. They receive only the
permuted matrix, right-hand side, and generic variable names. Neither
receives generator identities, k, arm labels, block counts, or ground truth.

**E1 static nonunit parser**: enumerate every remaining row pair. Its eligible
targets are variables with nonzero coefficients in both rows and global row
incidence exactly two. If exactly two targets are eligible, derive and check
the 2x2 candidate without a unit-coefficient restriction. Accept checked
candidates in ascending (row pair, target pair) order if they do not conflict
on a row or target. Materialize the resulting set once.

**E2 residual-incidence peeling**: start after local routing. At each step,
enumerate all remaining row pairs. An eligible target has nonzero
coefficients in both rows, original global incidence in {2,3,4}, and incidence
exactly two among remaining rows. Require exactly two eligible shared targets;
derive and check the 2x2 candidate. Choose the lexicographically smallest
checked candidate, reserve its rows and targets, and repeat until none can be
found. Materialize the entire accepted set once. The original-incidence cap
four is specific to the v0.0.41 generator and must be reported as such.

For either method, a failed joint materialization or verification rejects
the entire proposed reduction and returns the independently verified exact
full-system answer. Rejected proposals are not counted as saved work. No
exhaustive search, beam search, learned scorer, or post-hoc threshold tuning.

Reuse v0.0.41 arm definitions, 16 examples per (arm,k,n) cell, arms
COEFFICIENT/OVERLAP/COMBINED and (k,n) in {(2,16),(2,32),(4,16),(4,32)}.

Final seeds start at 1,010,000 + 1,000*k + n, plus fixed arm offsets
0/100,000/200,000; control seeds start at 1,310,000 + 1,000*k + n.
Validation fixtures use seeds outside those ranges. Reject duplicates and
signatures seen in v0.0.41 and all its predecessors. Freeze methods before

## Measurements and decisions

Report by arm and cell: verified retention, unsafe accepted reductions,
nonunit candidate visibility, mean verified solver-savings and elimination
recovery relative to the exact generator reference, accepted block-label
recall only as a diagnostic, mean exact solver arithmetic ops, row-pair
examinations, 2x2 derivation/checker calls, final materialization attempts,
and rejected joint materializations. Measure checker arithmetic, wall time,
memory, and energy separately before claiming total efficiency; solver
savings alone cannot establish it.

KEEP_AUDIT requires all 224 unique fresh systems; all 224 oracle references
verified; all 448 E1/E2 final answers verified; zero unsafe accepted
reductions; 16 examples in each of 14 cells; and no accepted reductions
on controls. A method may safely fall back and still satisfy KEEP_AUDIT.

For each active arm, label **DETERMINISTIC_SUFFICIENT** only if E2 achieves
at least 95% mean solver-savings recovery, at least 95% mean elimination
recovery, 100% verified retention and zero unsafe reductions. Otherwise
label **RESIDUAL_HEADROOM**. If COEFFICIENT E1 misses its declared block
row-target incidences, label **PARSER_REPAIR_FAILED**. If any oracle has no
positive solver savings, label **INVALID_FAMILY** for that cell. A headroom
result does not itself justify a neural model.

This family still exposes an engineered small-degree motif. Success cannot
establish optimal decomposition, total-compute savings, edge AI savings, or
transfer to natural-language and real engineering tasks.

### Cost and failure boundary

Across each 64-system active arm, E2 examined a mean of **1,305.5 row pairs
per system**. It made a mean of **58 exact 2x2 derivation/checker calls** on
COEFFICIENT and **9.5** on each overlap arm. These checks and the local router
are outside the final solver arithmetic-op savings above. There is no
total-compute or energy-efficiency conclusion. A cheaper index or
representation should be tested before optimizing a learned scorer.

In the 32 no-reduction controls, the two methods each proposed invalid
whole-system 2x2 reductions on 16 of the (2,2) controls. The joint
materializer rejected all 32 proposals (16 per method), and each returned
the verified full solve with zero accepted reductions. This is safe but
wasted discovery/checking work and should be eliminated by an explicit
minimum-retained-dimension guard in the next version.

The generator supplies sparse low-incidence blocks with an acyclic chain.
E2's original-incidence cap four and residual peeling exploit that structure.
The next harder test must add high-incidence distractors and ambiguous
alternative decompositions after measuring and reducing discovery cost.

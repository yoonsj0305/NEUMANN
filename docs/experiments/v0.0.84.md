# v0.0.84 — Column-scale-invariant classical basis portfolio

Status: preregistered design; no v084 generated-case solve, timing or training.
Base: v083 main `63b9404707ad3e185ff2b70a8188ed9171ea102d`.
The v082/v083 first archives are unchanged and will not be rerun.

## Hypothesis and limits

v083's raw column-norm shortcut vanished under column normalization. A richer
classical portfolio using normalized coefficients, cost residuals, primal
scores and rank-revealing QR may recover verified basis candidates cheaply.
This is an opened, generator-aware development screen, not blind/natural LP
evidence or an exhaustive test of classical methods. These are ordinary
linear-algebra heuristics, not learned NEUMANN contributions.

## Fixed inputs and observable-only policy

Use the unchanged v082 family and full grid: m=32/64/128, condition=1/1000,
two replicates, width=1/16; fresh seeds 84100..84111. Raw and corresponding
column-normalized forms yield 48 cases. Only A,b,c enter the proposer.
It always pays normalization: d_j=||A_j||, D=A/d, q=c/d. Positive column
rescaling of A,c cancels algebraically; floating-point rounding and pivot/tie
sensitivity prevent a claim of bit-identical candidate selection everywhere.
Zero/non-finite columns are rejected, not silently treated as normalized.

Build all candidates before attempting any; charge the entire build even if
the first candidate wins. Stable index ties; deduplicate unordered bases.
Fixed order, at most seven distinct m-column proposals:

1. Fit D.T*y≈q using SciPy lstsq, gelsy, cond=1e-12. Rank |q-D.T*y| ascending;
   propose first m. Refit on the lowest residual n/2, n/4, n/8, then m columns
   (each clipped to at least m), rerank all columns and propose first m after
   each of four fits. No observed result tunes the round count or cutoff.
2. Solve minimum-norm D*z≈b with the same driver/cutoff. Rank z descending,
   propose first m. On the first min(n,2m) columns, use economic QR with
   pivoting, propose its first m pivot columns.

Solve each candidate on the original received A,b,c, reconstruct full primal
and dual and run the unchanged v081 numerical certificate. Charge failed
proposals, all verification and original native `highs` fallback. Proposal
errors are recorded and fall back rather than disappearing from accounting.
No seed/spec/form/condition, oracle basis, solver answer or trained parameter
is an admissible proposal input. Sequential LU and numerical verification
are not exact formal proof.

Implementation references (no algorithmic novelty claim):
- https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.linalg.lstsq.html
- https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.linalg.qr.html

## Routes and complete costs

Native highs/highs-ds/highs-ipm retain presolve, threads=1, parallel=False,
5s solver limit, and identical certificate checking. New portfolio is a
classical Direct route. Frozen v083 norm discovery is a sensitivity control,
not part of the native reference and not a neural ablation.
Report every route, not just the winner. Best eligible native median per case
is a post-hoc strong diagnostic comparator, not deployable free routing.
One warmup plus three serial shuffled repeats for each of five routes and
48 cases: 240 warmups, 720 timed observations. Schedule seed 84991.

Complete wall latency includes normalization, all least squares/QR, proposals,
LU, reconstruction, verifier, failed work and fallback. Dataset generation,
archive serialization and offline witness replay are separate setup/storage
costs, not hidden speed claims. No memory, energy or learned-inference claim.
The fixed heuristic budget bounds work, not a hard real-time deadline.

## Capability and frozen decisions

Both forms must have an eligible native and portfolio path on all cases
(warmup and all repeats), else CAPABILITY_UNREACHED. A failure of the v083
control is retained but does not hide the new route's capability.
For each form use its 12 expanded cases. Require geometric mean complete-cost
ratio ≤0.50, at least 8/12 ratios ≤0.80, and at least 8/12 never falling back.
Both forms must pass for CLASSICAL_PORTFOLIO_CONSUMES_MATCHED_HEADROOM.
Raw-only pass: RAW_ONLY_CLASSICAL_GAIN. Otherwise:
RESIDUAL_HEADROOM_UNRESOLVED_NOT_LEARNING_ADMISSION.
No outcome authorizes neural training or closes Q3/Q4. These engineering
thresholds are not confidence bounds or statistical power calculations.

## Reproducibility and first-record discipline

Pin Python 3.11.x, NumPy 2.4.6, SciPy 1.17.1, threadpoolctl 3.7.0; one-thread
environment and threadpool limits, OPENBLAS_CORETYPE=HASWELL. Record actual
runtime/threadpool metadata during the audit, not retroactively.
Retain exact A,b,c arrays for each case and x,y witnesses for every accepted
candidate/native path in the first gzip JSON. Never save oracle metadata.
Hashes use original float64 dtype, shape and array bytes. Offline validation
recomputes certificate checks from retained inputs/witnesses without solving
or regenerating inputs; BLAS-dependent source regeneration is optional only.
It also checks protocol, route/repeat coverage, complete costs and summary.
Do not substitute accepted flags for witness verification.
Exclusive output reservation before timing; a failed attempt is retained,
never overwritten or repeated opportunistically. Publish protocol and tested
implementation before any generated-case solve/timing. Ordinary CI runs
fixtures and offline integrity checks only, never a final timing audit.

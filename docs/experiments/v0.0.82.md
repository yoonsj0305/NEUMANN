# v0.0.82 — Zero-discovery LP basis headroom screen

Status: **pre-registered development mechanism screen**. Protocol, generator,
comparators, seeds and decision thresholds are fixed before the first retained
timing audit. No model is trained in this version.

## Research question

If the exact optimal LP basis/support were supplied for free, would enough
**complete verified compute headroom** remain to justify any later attempt to
discover that structure?

The screen compares:

1. a deliberately strong Direct diagnostic: the per-case fastest verified
   median among SciPy/HiGHS `highs`, `highs-ds`, and `highs-ipm`;
2. a non-deployable oracle route that receives the exact optimal basis for free,
   factors only that basis, reconstructs the full primal/dual witness, and then
   pays the identical v0.0.81 original-LP verifier.

Both sides receive the same raw observable `A,b,c`. Only the oracle diagnostic
receives the hidden basis indices. The best-of-three Direct choice is selected
after measurement with zero routing cost, so it is also non-deployable and
intentionally favors the Direct lower bound.

A positive result is **not** Q3 or Q4 and is not evidence that a learned basis
predictor is useful. It only says that the residual structural decision may be
worth studying.

## Why controls are mandatory

Exploratory design checks before this protocol showed that a known-basis linear
algebra path can beat a generic LP solver even when `n=m`, where there is no
variable-count compression at all. Therefore raw oracle/HiGHS speedup would
confound:

- generic solver overhead / algorithm specialization, and
- value from removing nonbasic variables.

Every latent problem pair therefore contains:

- control: `n=m`;
- expanded: `n=16m`;

with the same underlying basis matrix, positive basic solution, right-hand
side, and dual vector. The experiment must show that Direct cost grows with
width materially more than the oracle path, not merely that LU is cheaper than
a generic LP call.

## Frozen constructed family

Rows:

`m ∈ {32, 64, 128}`

Basis condition numbers:

`κ(B) ∈ {1, 1000}`

Width factors:

`n/m ∈ {1, 16}`

Two matched replicates are generated for each `(m, κ)`, producing:

- 12 latent pairs;
- 24 observable LP cases.

Seeds begin at `82100` and are fixed by pair. Control and expanded members of
one pair share all latent basis-side quantities before nonbasic columns are
added.

The optimal basis is constructed with prescribed singular values. Basic
variables are strictly positive. Expanded cases add random nonbasic columns
with strictly positive reduced costs in `[0.25, 2.0)`, so the planted basic
solution is nondegenerate and uniquely optimal under the generated standard
form. Columns are then randomly permuted.

This is author-generated opened development data, not a natural workload,
independent benchmark, or final generalization set.

## Verification authority

All accepted candidates must pass
`verify_standard_form_certificate` from v0.0.81 against the **original**
`A,b,c`.

The verifier checks:

- primal equality feasibility;
- primal non-negativity;
- dual feasibility;
- primal/dual objective agreement;

with the frozen componentwise float64 backward-error contract. Complementarity
is diagnostic only.

Neither a solver status, planted answer, reduced model, nor basis metadata can
replace the original verifier.

## Direct comparator

Frozen methods:

- `highs`
- `highs-ds`
- `highs-ipm`

Each uses presolve and a 5 second solver time limit. A Direct method is eligible
for a case only when its warmup and all three timed repetitions are independently
verified. The per-case fastest eligible median is used as the Direct diagnostic
lower bound.

If no Direct method is eligible for a case, capability is unreached. A timeout
or invalid certificate is never treated as speedup evidence.

## Oracle-basis path

The oracle receives only the exact basis index vector in addition to the raw
problem. It must still pay for:

- basis extraction from the original matrix;
- one LU factorization;
- primal basis solve;
- transposed dual solve using the same LU factors;
- full primal reconstruction;
- full original-LP v0.0.81 verification.

The oracle does **not** pay basis-discovery cost. Therefore it is an optimistic
upper-bound diagnostic, not a deployable NEUMANN path.

## Timing protocol

- one untimed warmup per route and case;
- three timed repetitions per route and case;
- four routes per case: three Direct methods plus oracle basis;
- 24 cases, hence 288 retained timed observations;
- serial shuffled schedule with fixed seed `82991`;
- frozen runtime: Python 3.11.x, NumPy 2.4.6, SciPy 1.17.1,
  threadpoolctl 3.7.0;
- single-thread requirement:
  `OMP_NUM_THREADS=OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`;
- `threadpoolctl` additionally limits supported numerical libraries to one
  thread;
- the audit runner fails closed on runtime-version or thread-boundary drift.

Input generation, source hashing, latent provenance hashing and condition-number
measurement are recorded separately and are not included in per-query warm
latency. The benchmark assumes `A,b,c` are already the received problem.

No energy, FLOP, peak-memory or dollar claim is permitted from this audit.

## Frozen admission rule

All capability requirements above must hold. Then:

1. geometric mean of
   `oracle complete median / best-Direct complete median`
   across the 12 expanded cases must be `<= 0.50`;
2. at least 8/12 expanded cases must show at least 20% complete-cost reduction
   (`ratio <= 0.80`);
3. for each matched pair define:

   `Direct growth = Direct(n=16m) / Direct(n=m)`

   `Oracle growth = Oracle(n=16m) / Oracle(n=m)`

   `Scaling amplification = Direct growth / Oracle growth`

   at least 8/12 pairs must have scaling amplification `>= 2.0`.

These are engineering admission thresholds, not a statistical power analysis.

Pass decision:

`ADMIT_BASIS_DISCOVERY_SEARCH_NOT_MODEL_TRAINING`

Fail decision:

`REJECT_BASIS_DISCOVERY_TRAINING`

Capability failure:

`CAPABILITY_UNREACHED`

A pass authorizes only a **deterministic discovery / shortcut screen** next.
It does not authorize model training yet.

## Exploration disclosure

Before freezing this protocol, development exploration examined several dense
constructed LP sizes and exposed two design issues:

1. raw oracle-vs-HiGHS speedup exists even in no-compression controls;
2. the first v0.0.81 global residual scaling could falsely reject a larger
   numerically valid HiGHS solution.

Those observations motivated the matched `n=m` controls, scaling-amplification
gate, and the v0.0.81 backward-error hardening. The final v0.0.82 seeds have not
been used for a retained audit at protocol freeze, but this experiment is still
an **opened development screen**, not an independent holdout, because the
family and dimensions were selected after exploration.

## Next step if admitted

Do **not** immediately train a neural basis predictor.

First compare against cheap deterministic and classical structure discovery:

- obvious active/nonbasic tests available from raw observables;
- presolve and scaling information available to Direct under equal authority;
- basis/warm-start heuristics and applicable classical optimization methods;
- any strong prior work that predicts active sets or LP bases.

Only residual headroom that survives those baselines can motivate Q3 learned
discovery. Q4 would still require a matched runnable Direct learner,
NEUMANN-without-compression ablation, equal training budget, fresh held-out
structures, full failure/fallback accounting and training amortization.

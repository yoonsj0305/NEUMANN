# v0.0.82 — Zero-model LP basis/support headroom screen

Status: protocol frozen before the first timed audit. Constructed development
family only. No model is trained in this version.

## One question

If a non-deployable diagnostic were given the exact optimal LP basis/support
at zero discovery cost, could it reduce the complete verified path by at
least 20% against strong Direct comparisons?

If even that diagnostic cannot clear the gate, reject basis/active-set learning
on this family before training.

A positive result is only mechanism headroom. It does not show that the basis
is inferable from admissible input, that learning is necessary, that the
family is natural, or that NEUMANN beats a matched model.

## Frozen family

Standard-form LP:

```
minimize      c^T x
subject to    A x = b
              x >= 0
```

Twelve deterministic dense float64 cases use rows 16, 32, or 64; columns 16m
or 64m; and two independent seeds per size. Maximum size is 64 x 4096.

Each case plants a well-conditioned orthonormal m x m basis, randomly permutes
its locations, chooses a strictly positive basic solution, and constructs a
dual-feasible objective with strictly positive nonbasic reduced costs. The
planted witness is construction/audit information only. It is not the
original-task verifier and may not enter any future deployable proposal.

These are author-generated constructed cases, not Netlib, MIPLIB, production
LPs, or evidence of natural active-set incidence.

## Comparator paths

Three fixed Direct paths use SciPy HiGHS with presolve enabled:

1. `highs`
2. `highs-ds`
3. `highs-ipm`

The diagnostic `oracle_basis` path receives the exact optimal basis for free,
solves only the square basis system and its transpose, reconstructs full
primal/dual witnesses, then pays the same original-LP verifier.

For a conservative reference, the primary gate compares the basis diagnostic
with the fastest measured Direct policy per case among the three HiGHS modes.
The strongest single fixed Direct policy is reported separately.

## Shared capability authority

Every successful path must pass the v0.0.81 original-coefficient primal/dual
certificate with frozen atol=1e-8 and rtol=1e-8. A solver success flag alone is
insufficient. Complementarity remains a diagnostic under the v0.0.81 contract.
If any warmup or timed path fails the certificate, the result is
CAPABILITY_UNREACHED and no speed ratio is admitted.

## Timing

- one warmup per policy/case, retained separately;
- five timed repetitions per policy/case;
- all 240 timed observations retained;
- serial shuffled order, seed 8291;
- Direct solver time limit 10 s per call;
- basis extraction/copy, square solves, reconstruction and original
  verification are all charged to the diagnostic path;
- Direct solver plus original verification are charged to each Direct path.

Input generation is outside warm inference time but source hashes are stored.
No energy, peak-memory, training-cost or deployment claim follows.

## Frozen admission rule

Let D_i be the fastest measured Direct median on case i, and O_i the
oracle-basis median. Advance only if all cases verify and both conditions hold:

```
mean_i(O_i) / mean_i(D_i) <= 0.80
count_i[O_i <= 0.80 D_i] >= 9 of 12
```

On pass:
`MECHANISM_HEADROOM_PRESENT_MODEL_TRAINING_NOT_YET_ADMITTED`

On fail:
`REJECT_BASIS_DISCOVERY_TRAINING_ON_THIS_CONSTRUCTED_FAMILY`

The thresholds are engineering choices, not statistical-power results.

## Boundary

A pass still does not justify training. A later gate must test whether
basis/support has an observable non-oracle signal and whether a learned
proposer beats cheap deterministic tests, warm starts, a matched Direct
executable/warm-start learner, and the NEUMANN no-compression ablation.

Prior art already includes learned initial-basis selection and learned
active-set or dual warm-starts. A later NEUMANN claim must concern complete
verified structural-compression economics, not invention of basis prediction.

Q3 and Q4 remain OPEN regardless of this screen.

# v0.0.48 — Cost-gate opportunity audit before model training

Status: pre-registered and evaluator frozen in `9ef9a18` before the first
complete audit. F1 (v0.0.43) and F3 (v0.0.46) remain frozen.
No model is trained in this step. The question is whether any useful
decision boundary remains after a cheap deterministic gate, not whether
a classifier can fit synthetic generator labels.

## Testable claim

At matched dimension 16, a high-incidence sparse family can benefit from
F3 while a dense exact family and uncompressible controls may favor F1.
However the observable original column-incidence histogram may separate
the arms perfectly. Training a model on that shortcut would add cost
without measuring learned structural intelligence.

## Fresh data and comparators

128 fresh exact integer systems at n=16: HIGH_DEGREE k=2 and k=4, 24
each; DENSE k=12, 48; CONTROL k=16, 32. Use disjoint new seeds starting
at 3,480,000 and reject a reused signature from the v0.0.46 audit or
within this audit. The high-incidence mechanism remains the v0.0.44
intervention; dense examples are block-triangular, with a 12x12
diagonally dominant core and a 4x4 diagonally dominant coupled block,
nonzero lower-left coupling, and random row/column permutation. This
guarantees invertibility before permutation. Controls have n=k=16 and
are independently generated fully dense, strictly diagonally dominant
integer matrices; the frozen mixed generator does not offer this cell.

Compare frozen F1 and F3 on the same examples. Fit the frozen v0.0.33
scorer once before timing. Measure three alternating paired repetitions
of each full path (routing, discovery, checking, execution,
reconstruction and original verification). Exclude data generation,
scorer fit and benchmark-only baseline solve. Require identical verified
exact answers and no unsafe accepted reduction. Report median within-case
F3/F1 ratio, not ratio of arm medians. A robust F3 win is ratio <=0.90;
a robust F1 win is ratio >=1.10. Cases in between are timing-neutral.
These cutoffs are measurement filters, not statistical guarantees.

Pre-choice features are dimension, nonzero count, and exact histogram of
original column incidences. Do not include family IDs, split tags,
oracle blocks, final answer, observed runtime or post-F3 cache metrics.
Compare two fixed routing policies: ALWAYS_F1 and a one-feature static
gate `F3 iff at least one column has original incidence exactly six`.
Report gate decisions, robust-win coverage and errors. Gate inference
time must be measured as part of its route's full-path time. It can
abstain only to verified F1; any failed F3 verification also falls back
to verified F1 and counts the failed work.

## Decision

`OPPORTUNITY_VISIBLE` only if the exact safety/freshness conditions hold,
both F1 and F3 have at least 16 robust wins each, and at least 16 cases
where the cheaper measured route conflicts with the static gate's route.
Otherwise `NO_LEARNED_GATE_JUSTIFIED` (or `INVALID_FAMILY` on failed
safety/freshness). Even an opportunity is not a model performance claim:
timing labels can be noisy, and a learned model in a following version
must beat this static gate on held-out generator mechanisms including its
feature/inference cost. Do not promote a model solely on this dataset.

No energy, peak memory or real-workload claims. This is a synthetic local
cost feasibility check, not a 10x gain or proof of general intelligence.

## First and only complete audit

The [raw summary](results/v048_first_audit.json) records 128/128 distinct,
fresh, exact systems with both paths and the static gate verifying the
same original answers. No gate fallback was needed. The paired medians
and robust-win counts were:

| Arm | Count | F3/F1 full-path median | Gate/F1 full-path median | Robust F1 / F3 / neutral |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| High-degree | 48 | 0.881 | 0.893 | 1 / 30 / 17 |
| Dense | 48 | 1.007 | 1.016 | 2 / 1 / 45 |
| Control | 32 | 0.997 | 0.991 | 3 / 3 / 26 |

Across all systems, 6 were robust F1 wins, 34 robust F3 wins and 88
neutral under the pre-registered 10% filter. Only five robust winners
conflicted with the static gate; all high-degree systems had an original
degree-six column and none of the dense/control systems did. Decision:
**NO_LEARNED_GATE_JUSTIFIED**. This is not proof that learning cannot help
on other families. The few opposite-arm robust wins could reflect local
timing noise; no repeat audit, model fit or threshold tuning follows this
result. The next family must break the original-incidence shortcut and
show independently verified residual headroom before fitting a model.

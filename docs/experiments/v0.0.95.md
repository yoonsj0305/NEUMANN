# v0.0.95 — Graph refinement adds no stable incremental basis value

## Frozen question

v0.0.94 established that the CG5 point-only route and the compact graph route
start from the same retained 2m column set. v0.0.95 therefore removes timing,
new inference and solver behavior from the question and asks only whether the
already-frozen graph reordering adds enough exact-m basis information to justify
another paid graph experiment.

The gate was published before the first replay in
[v0.0.95-preregistration.md](v0.0.95-preregistration.md). The frozen head used
by the first CI replay was `2e5000096e7fd1c836ae3a1f37057469555cbce6`.
No fitting, model inference, solver call, new input, timing rerun, checkpoint
selection or final holdout was performed. The replay reads the byte-exact
v0.0.94 first probe and its v0.0.88 parent sources only.

## Frozen gate

Both seeds had to satisfy all five conditions:

- exact-m original-LP certificate gain over point-only >=4/48,
- mean hidden-label basis-recall gain >=+0.05 absolute,
- at least 12/48 paired labelled-basis hit wins,
- at most 6/48 paired losses,
- identical retained 2m sets on 48/48 cases.

Hidden labels are diagnostic only. Original-LP certificate acceptance remains
the stronger authority because a problem may admit more than one optimal basis.

Neither seed passes. Seed87001 is directionally worse on both certificate count

improve recall on many cases, but its certificate gain is only +1, its mean
recall gain is +0.0234 rather than +0.05, and it loses 10 cases rather than the
allowed maximum of 6. The conjunction therefore fails without any threshold
change or favorable seed selection.

Decision:

`STOP_GRAPH_REFINEMENT_INFORMATION_CANDIDATE`

## Interpretation boundary

This result closes only the current **graph refinement on the frozen CG5 2m
shortlist**. It does not say graph networks can never help LP basis discovery.
It also does not establish a timing or energy result: no new timing was run,
and v0.0.94's contaminated timings remain unusable for a clean speed claim.

The important system result is narrower and useful. On this fixed target, the
extra graph computation does not produce stable incremental basis information
over the point-only selector large enough to justify another paid graph screen.
The same 2m retained sets are confirmed on all 96 seed-case pairs, so state
reduction alone cannot be credited to the graph.

Do not retrain or time another graph-refinement variant on these opened inputs.
Native remains default. Q3 and Q4 remain OPEN. The next LP step, if retained,
should test the cheaper point-only discovery path or a genuinely different
principled target under a separately frozen complete-cost protocol rather than
continue adding graph work.

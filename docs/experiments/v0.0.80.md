# v0.0.80 — Exact relational planning headroom screen

Status: protocol fixed before database construction, query timing or training.
This is a constructed engineering screen, not a real SQL workload sample.

## One question and fixed admission rule

Can even a non-deployable, zero-model-cost choice among the declared plans
reduce complete verified query cost by >=20% against the strongest fixed
cheap Direct policy? If not, do not train a planner on this bounded family.

Require all timed calls to agree exactly with a separately executed SQLite
original query, mean per-case oracle total / strongest fixed Direct total
<=0.8, and >=4/12 cases with >=20% total savings. These thresholds are
engineering assumptions, not observed results or a power calculation.
Timeouts/mismatches are CAPABILITY_UNREACHED, never speedup evidence.
The oracle is selected after measurement and excludes learned planning;
it is a diagnostic lower-bound candidate, not deployable policy or Q3/Q4.

## Workload and authority

Author-generated deterministic integer relations A(k,flag), B(k,j,w), and
C(j,flag), with 2,000 or 8,000 rows per table. Three distributions: uniform,
skewed keys, and flag/key correlation. Two filter thresholds: 25 and 75 out
of 100. Keys range 0..511, weights 1..9, no NULLs, signed-overflow exposure,
floats, non-deterministic SQL, outer joins or external user SQL.
12 cases, generator seeds starting 8001, 3 timed repetitions/policy/case.
Return exact COUNT(*) and SUM(B.w), with empty sums normalized to zero.
Input relations, generator/version and query definition remain observable
to all future learners; no performance label or answer may enter proposals.
No external benchmark data or query text is copied. These are not TPC-H,
JOB, production queries, or evidence of general-language intelligence.

Direct policies: DuckDB 1.5.6 native optimizers with ANALYZE; deterministic
bag-multiplicity preaggregation. The latter groups A/C by their join keys,
groups B by both keys, and computes counts/weight sums using multiplicity
products. It is available to Direct, not reserved for NEUMANN.
Four connected left-deep relation orders are diagnostic alternatives;
only join_order/build_side_probe_side are disabled for forcing, restored
after every call. All other native optimizer passes stay enabled.

SQLite supplies a separately implemented engine evaluating the original
join query, with indexes on join and filter columns. Every timed path pays
for this original-query verification. Its recomputation is deliberately
visible; gold lookup is not a free production verifier. Shared verification
cost may erase plan-selection opportunity. SQLite is a trusted implementation,
not formally verified software; exact agreement on this finite corpus is
not proof for all DBMS/input behavior.

## Timing and failure

One untimed correctness warmup per policy/case, retained separately.
Three repetitions, shuffled serial schedule seed 8091. Record every SQL
string, source table hashes, compile/control/execute, independent check and
complete wall time. Also preserve database setup/index/statistics time,
versions and hardware. Warmup and setup are separate from warm latency.
Per query: 2-second engine deadline using DuckDB interrupt and SQLite
progress callbacks, plus 512 MB DuckDB memory cap and one DuckDB thread.
All exceptions/mismatches are retained; do not delete or retune a bad cell.
No energy, memory-peak or model-inference claim is possible from this screen.

## Prior art and model consequence

Bao (https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03814) already learns per-query hints over
an existing optimizer. Balsa (https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.01441) and its DuckDB
adaptation (https://duckdb.org/library/balsa-duckdb-cross-architecture/)
are relevant learned-plan predecessors. Robust Predicate Transfer
(https://duckdb.org/library/robust-predicate-transfer/) is a strong
deterministic alternative for acyclic joins; a positive screen cannot
promote learning before comparing applicable mature alternatives.
Gremlin (https://duckdb.org/library/generation-of-adversarial-queries-via-bayesian-optimization/)
illustrates deliberate headroom construction; such selection must never be
presented as a representative natural workload or independent final set.
Reuse here is ideas/method definitions only, no copied code/data/weights.

If this screen passes, a **separate** preregistration must name runnable
matched models, equal supervision/tool access, no-compression ablation,
training/teacher budgets and fresh final cases. Join-order improvement alone
is planning evidence, not certified structural freedom reduction. If the
cheap multiplicity rewrite wins, credit the deterministic algebraic method.
Do not fit a model merely to rename an operation already done cheaply.

Frozen decision: `REJECT_BOUNDED_RELATIONAL_PLANNER_TRAINING`.
No learner, frontier comparison, peak-memory, energy or Q3/Q4 result follows.
Do not generalize this rejection to larger/cyclic queries, alternative plans
outside the finite six-policy set, or all learned query optimization.

The multiplicity rewrite is exact classical algebra and available to Direct.
It is not evidence that learned compression is needed. Re-executing the
original problem dominated this particular verification contract; the next
task must have an independently checkable witness cheaper than independently
resolving the task. Such a contract must be fixed before new measurements,
remain equally available to Direct, and must not replace checks by a free
answer lookup or waive the original capability requirement. This is the
next design decision, rather than training a planner on the rejected cases.

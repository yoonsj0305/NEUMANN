# v0.0.65 — Persistent LP basis diagnostic on SEMI3

Status: **negative, nonblind mechanism experiment**. SEMI3 adds a third
scenario to the already inspected SEMI core. It is correlated with SEMI2 and
is not an independent holdout. This experiment changes one mechanism in the
classical v0.0.64 cut path: each scenario retains its HiGHS feasibility and
optimality LP and updates row bounds between first-stage decisions. The LP
basis stays available. The master and cut policy remain the same. No neural
component, novel compression witness, independent dual-certificate checker,
or fixed-capability advantage is established.

The preceding scratch microbenchmark used one zero decision, a native
incumbent's first-stage decision, and six nearby decisions, across two
scenarios. Keeping the LPs reduced the eight paired recourse-evaluation
times from 28.74 to 7.04 seconds in that particular sequence (including
model construction). That sequence is nonblind, its incumbent acquisition
was not charged, and several nearby decisions yield the same recourse
violation. It only motivated the full-path check; it is **not** a system
speedup measurement. The scratch probe is not an official result artifact.

At iteration 8 of the first SEMI3 run, the master returned a warning when a
cut included a near-zero coefficient. The implementation now deletes any
coefficient of absolute size at most `1e-9` only when both variable bounds
are finite, and relaxes the inequality RHS by the worst possible contribution
of the removed term. A test checks the implication at box corners and rejects
an unbounded variable. The v0.0.64 replay code was updated with this
numerical correction; its archived result remains the historical initial
execution and was not replaced.

The recorded SEMI3 diagnostic uses a nominal 20-second complete-path budget,
including hash-checked archive parsing, construction, master and recourse
solves, and any original-model verification. It completed in 19.722 seconds,
made 24 master iterations and 103 recourse LP runs from five LP constructions,
and generated 41 feasibility plus 31 optimality cuts. Scenario 1 remained
recourse-infeasible at the final master proposal (elastic violation 0.4491).
There was **no original-feasible candidate**. Master objective 89 is not a
valid original objective. The native diagnostic, with the same nominal
20-second allowance including parsing, returned an original-verified feasible
objective 2919.08655 at 19.049 seconds; original normalized primal violation
was 1.33e-12 and integer violation zero. Native did not prove optimality
(reported gap 46.35%). These are single descriptive runs with different
stopping reserves, not a paired latency distribution. Because the candidate
gave no accepted answer, no iso-capability ratio exists.

**Decision:** basis reuse removes LP reconstruction work but does not resolve
the slow search for a first-stage decision feasible in every scenario. Keep
native HiGHS as the default for this family. Stop tuning this SEMI core. The
next core experiment should identify a specific structural reduction with a
verifiable witness and a separate, genuinely unseen family before freezing
an iso-capability total-cost protocol. A faster classical Benders loop alone
would not establish NEUMANN's structural-compression thesis.

## What Q3 now means

Q3 is closed as `PASS_LP_MECHANISM`, not because the learned model predicts
the exact optimal basis. It does not need to.

The frozen target-free proposer recovers a support that lets restricted native
execution retain at least 80% of the available Oracle structural savings in
every fresh cell, with verifier-controlled expansion and safe fallback.

That is sufficient structural discovery authority for the next system question.

## What Q4 now means

Q4 is closed as `PASS_LP_MECHANISM` because the same mechanism is economical
under the preregistered complete-cost accounting:

- discovery burden stays far below 20%;
- complete amortized cost stays far below Direct;
- training amortization is charged;
- failed attempts and fallback rights are charged;
- verification authority is identical.

This is stronger than a solver-only or proposal-only speed comparison.

## Next boundary

Do not continue local Q3/Q4 tuning on this LP line.

The frozen mechanism advances to **Q5**. Q5 must now test whether the mechanism
survives scale, harder/open-set distributions, and eventually materially
different task families while preserving complete verified economics.

A Q5 failure may limit the scope of the mechanism, but it does not retroactively
change the preregistered Q3/Q4 closure on this holdout.

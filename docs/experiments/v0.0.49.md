# v0.0.49 — Matched-incidence topology counterfactual

Status: pre-registered before the first complete audit. This is a
counterfactual family and cost-opportunity test, not model training.
Frozen F1 (v0.0.43), F3 (v0.0.46) and v0.0.48's one-feature static gate
are unchanged.

## Intervention and constraints

Starting from a new high-degree example, form a matched second system:
move each target's two donor-row nonzeros into two previously zero
retained core rows, retaining each target's original degree six and all
coefficient values. Recompute the right-hand side from the same ground
truth. The two systems must have identical dimension, total nonzero count
and sorted original column-incidence histogram; all are available before
choosing a solver. Their row placement differs. In the first system the
target may become a two-row residual candidate after peeling; in the
second it stays in the retained core. Verify full-rank behavior with an
exact original solve and ground-truth comparison, not the generator label.

## Freshness, measurement and controls

96 matched pairs: (k,n)=(2,16),(2,32),(4,16),(4,32), 24 per cell, for
192 distinct systems. New seeds start at 3,620,000; contract fixtures
use separate seeds beginning at 3,610,000. Reject signatures reused
within v0.0.49 or any prior v0.0.46/v0.0.48 final audit. Freeze method,
generator, evaluator, thresholds and all contracts before the first
complete final run. Do not train or tune on these paired final systems.

For each system compare frozen F1, F3 and the static gate with three
alternating repetitions each. Timing includes gate features if gated,
routing, candidate search, materialization or fallback, retained solve,
reconstruction and full original verification. Exclude scorer fit,
generation and benchmark-only independent baseline solve. Require all
answers to verify, both members to match the cheap features exactly,
no unsafe reduction, and F3's retained dimension to be two lower in
the recoverable member than in the retained-support member. Report
per-member paired median F3/F1 and gate/F1 ratios, and the within-pair
ratio difference. Use a 10% margin: robust F3 if <=0.90, robust F1 if
>=1.10, otherwise timing-neutral. This is a noise filter only.

`OPPORTUNITY_VISIBLE` requires all 96 valid matched structural pairs,
at least 16 pairs with a robust F3 win in the recoverable member *and*
a robust F1 win in the retained-support member, and at least 16 robust
static-gate mistakes in the retained-support members. Otherwise record
`STRUCTURAL_CONTRAST_NO_COST_HEADROOM`, or `INVALID_FAMILY` on failed
matching/safety/structural contracts. Even an opportunity is not model
evidence; a later learned selector must beat topology-aware deterministic
baselines on held-out mechanisms including feature and inference cost.

Potential shortcuts remain: row-degree distribution and exact two-row
postings can be computed deterministically. A learned classifier must
not be credited for rediscovering a cheaper exact test. Degraded mode is
verified F1 on failed F3, and failed work remains in the timing. No
energy, memory, real workload or 10x claim follows from this audit.

# v0.0.88 — preregistered first actual model study

Status: NOT FITTED, FINAL INPUTS UNOPENED at registration. This is a bounded
constructed-LP experiment, not a test of general/frontier reasoning efficiency.
v087 admitted this roster-specific budget under a nondeployable perfect-output
diagnostic. Its missing confounded first archive remains a disclosed limitation.

## Frozen models and fit

compact16/full16 use the exact v087 parameterization, same seeded initialization,
5,156 parameters each. Only compact16 removes states/edges after update one.
Direct includes full16, point16 (611 parameters), full128 (299,268 parameters),
each at seeds 87001 and 87002. These are budget-adapted candidates informed by
Fan et al. (ICML 2023), NOT trained reproductions of published baselines; Lu et
al. (ICML 2025) remains necessary before global learned-LP claims.

All eight checkpoints receive the SAME 48 normalized fresh planted examples,
rows 32/64, condition 1/1000, width16, seeds 88100–88147. Basis/primal/dual
labels are generated only for supervision and checked against the original LP.
All receive the same eight paid observable features and deterministic order.
12 epochs, Adam lr .001, zero weight decay, maximum 240s/model. Last epoch only;
no validation search, retries, seed selection, extra labels or tuned stopping.
Balanced coarse BCE + selected refined BCE + .1*(scaled primal MSE + dual MSE).
Scaling transforms x to normalized-column coordinates and y by observable cost
RMS. Actual setup/feature/label and fit costs are retained, not treated as free.

## Prospective withheld final generation

Final inputs must NOT be generated until all eight fits are settled and saved.
12 cases, seeds 88900–88911, alternating conditions 1/1000. Six new m64 cases
are IID; six m128 cases are size+surface shift with signed row permutation and
positive column scaling exp(U[-1.5,1.5]). This changes representation and size,
not the planted generative structure; no natural/domain generalization claim.
No final target enters observable features, learned forward or execution.
All exact train/final arrays and original witnesses are retained; offline replay
must use those bytes, never regenerate using a potentially different BLAS.

## Equal authority and complete cost

Every learner may try its predicted x/y answer, then its predicted top-m basis,
original LP LU/check, native simplex warm repair and cold rescue. Direct and
no-compression share this adapter; full16 serves both roles with ONE checkpoint,
not two renamed models. Proposal/feature/tensor/forward/head decoding, rejected
answer verification, LU, original check, failures and fallback share a 5s original
deadline. Classical comparison includes seven v087 real routes PLUS the entire
v084 seven-candidate portfolio with charged checking/fallback. No oracle route.
Native full solve remains deployed default until a genuine gate passes.

One warmup and three timed repeats, deterministic random order 88991+repeat,
16 routes x 12 cases x 4 repeats = 768 calls. Use the same pinned v087 runtime,
single thread/HASWELL dispatch, with no concurrent tests or fitting during costs.
Do not redo the first fit or final timing for favorable results. Immutable
stage checkpoints preserve completed training and partial final observations.

## Fixed bounded candidate gate

All calls must pass original certificates before any ratio. For BOTH seeds and
BOTH final groups, compact complete-cost geomean must be <=.8 of (a) fastest
per-case classical, (b) fastest per-case noncompact learner across BOTH seeds,
(c) own full16 no-compression ablation. Each comparison requires >=4/6 cases
with >=20% savings. Compact top-m must certify without native on all repeats
in >=4/6 cases/group. Adding attributed train setup+compact fitting amortized
over 10,000 queries must also retain <=.8 classical ratio. Report break-even;
never assume a real deployment supplies that reuse volume.

Pass is only `BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_ROSTER_PASS_NOT_GLOBAL_Q3_Q4`; failure is
`FIRST_LEARNED_CANDIDATE_GATE_FAILED`, or capability/fit-budget failure. Global
Q3/Q4 remain OPEN in every outcome because published baselines and natural
problem coverage are missing. Preserve negative model results and do not
convert classical/oracle success into learned or general-model claims.

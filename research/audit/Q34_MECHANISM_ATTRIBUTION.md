family; it is1/128 in all16 retained BP optima. The width ratio m/n stays1/16,
so the old dimension channel alone does not describe this eightfold change.
The2m/4m executors retain16/32 times the positive BP support size. Yet shrinking

Under the original verifier's dual-feasibility tolerance, every retained BP
Native dual also has tight columns with zero primal mass:6..103 additional
columns across the16 cases. These are numerical diagnostic tightness sets,
not an exact basis or unique certificate claim. The original training witnesses
have none outside their positive basis. Thus answer-support labels and columns
relevant to original-certificate completion cease to coincide. The arbitrary
choice of one optimal basis/dual is not a universal sufficient representation.

This supports separating H1 target mismatch, H2 representation/cardinality,
H3 feature distribution and H4 certificate completion. It does not prove one
is the sole cause. The registered BP economics diagnostic already rejects
learning investment for its current adapter; another cheap coverage computation
does not reverse that decision. No new optimizer or timed rescue ablation is
needed to maintain **HOLD_LEARNING**.

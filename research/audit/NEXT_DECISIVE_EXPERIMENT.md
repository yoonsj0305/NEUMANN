# Next decisive experiment — opened BP certificate economics

2026-10-08 · Master Directive v2.0 Phase 2 · CPU only · local only.

## Decision changed by this experiment

Phase 1 established that some frozen proposals recover optimal primal but fail
original dual feasibility. Existing v104 provided optimal support AND dual free;
it did not isolate whether certification can economically be obtained without a
free dual. This is the remaining uncertainty that matters before an architecture
or learning investment. The user requested continued implementation after the
completed Phase 1 audit. This diagnostic is a distinct opened-development record;
it cannot replace M106, Q5, v104 or any fresh/global result.

## One hypothesis

H: variable-cardinality sufficient support can be executed and certified cheaply
without an Oracle dual, compared with the strongest declared native envelope.
Alternative explanations: fixed 2m/4m representation overhead; paid full-goal
certificate acquisition overhead. No new learned architecture is implemented.

## Decisions

- C and D both lack headroom: HOLD this representation/executor learning.
- D wins while C loses: fixed-cardinality representation is an actual contributor;
  at most one minimal sparse adapter may then be retained, no new neural model.
- D has headroom and B loses: discovery remains a potential target; first ensure
  the SPGL1/native discovery comparator also has a residual economic gap.
- OnlyE wins: full-goal certificate acquisition is the economic bottleneck.
- Strong specialist eliminates the gap: reuse that algorithm, HOLD learned
  replacement. No existing algorithm is called a NEUMANN invention.
- Missing/mismatched/late evidence: INCOMPLETE; preserve all first bytes.
